Option 1 – Ask us directly

  1. Send a message to our Facebook Page: facebook.com/427704297095129. You can also send a DM to our Instagram account.

Option 2 – Remove the app from your Facebook account

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
  2. Find HyperFrames App (if it is listed) and click Remove.

Messages and comments you sent on Facebook or Instagram are stored by Meta. You can delete them yourself at any time from your own account.
